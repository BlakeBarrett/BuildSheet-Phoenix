Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device by websites, apps, and online media. BuildSheet uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (like Dark Mode), authenticate your login session, and analyze platform traffic.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Service to function properly. They cannot be turned off. Without these, you cannot log in securely or save your Build Sheet offline (via IndexedDB/localStorage). We do not require consent for these.

Preferences Cookies

These allow us to remember your site settings, such as your selected theme (dark/light) or your accepted cookie policy preferences (`buildsheet-cookie-consent`).

Analytics & Performance Cookies

If you explicitly opt-in, we use analytics cookies (like Google Analytics) to help us understand how visitors interact with our Service. This allows us to improve performance and usability.

Marketing & Advertising Cookies

We do not currently use third-party tracking pixels or marketing cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. How to Control Your Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences directly on our website at any time using our preference center.


4. California "Do Not Sell" Rights

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data. BuildSheet does not sell your personal data. By rejecting non-essential cookies via our consent manager, you disable all third-party tracking that might be construed as "sharing."
